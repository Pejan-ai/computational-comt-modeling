# Computational Modeling of COMT-Mediated Estrogen Toxicity and Fibroid Risk

## Motivation
Estrogen metabolism involves complex, noisy, and partially observed biological processes. 
In prior experimental and wearable sensing work on COMT-mediated estrogen metabolism, 
I encountered fundamental limits to manual reasoning about variability, uncertainty, 
and downstream risk. This project explores how computational and probabilistic models 
can formalize these biological uncertainties into explicit, testable representations.

## Goal
To model how variation in COMT activity may influence estrogen metabolite accumulation 
and fibroid risk under uncertainty, using a combination of statistical, probabilistic, 
and neural modeling approaches.

## Status
Project initialization. Data generation and baseline models in progress.
